# M7 - The Definition Anchor

## Runtime operator contract

- Activation: a word, category, or equivocal definition blocks the underlying claim from being evaluated.
- Field target: the local semantic anchor needed for the current proposition, not the entire history of the term or a broader doctrinal essay.
- Burden/submove form: tactic `ⁿBᵢ[OPᵢ]`: identify the disputed term, propose or elicit the working definition needed for this claim, and bind the next operation to that definition.
- Δ effect: `ΔⁿB` changes an equivocal term into a bounded working proposition; `Δκ` releases M6, M1, M9, or object-level evaluation only if the anchoring actually removed the semantic block.
- Possible ∇ reread: after anchoring, check target-explicit `∇·B` for remaining definition pressure and `∇×κ` if the interlocutor keeps moving the definition to avoid the claim.
- R(H,Δ) obligation: reread agreement, counterdefinition, hidden broader-conception capture, and the held substantive route before continuing.
- Hold/release/closure effect: release only the semantic anchor and the next bounded proposition it licenses; hold wider disputes over contested terms unless the input makes them live.
- Output boundary: `layer-b-permitted` with output shapes `bounded-single-pass`. Default render may show compact state markers when control-bound; long formalism stays audit/formalism-expanded.
- Negative constraints: do not win by stipulative definition, do not smuggle the conclusion into the anchor, and do not turn a local term repair into full closure; no scalar closure, no proof-by-symbol, and no ∇ truth-or-warrant claim.
- Fixture/checker: Catalogue/frontmatter integrity is guarded by `tools/check_ttp_operator_contracts.py`; direct structural routing coverage remains a remediation item.


**Type:** Meta-tactic
**Deploy when:** The interlocutor challenges a word rather than engaging the underlying claim; a terminological dispute is being used to avoid substantive engagement.

Common forms: "religion is too wide a concept," "that is not what guidance means," "you are using 'reason' differently than I do."

This is either genuine need for clarification or a deflection tactic. Either way the procedure is the same.

## Four-step procedure

1. Acknowledge that terminology can be contested.
2. Offer a precise working definition for the current discussion.
3. Invite agreement or counter-proposal.
4. Proceed with the agreed or stipulated definition, noting explicitly that the argument still stands under it.

The key move is to show that the argument's force does not depend on the particular word chosen.

## Scope boundary

M7 is not the owner of broader conception-capture problems. When contradiction pressure is being generated by silent redefinition, public-language capture, universals/particulars confusion, or mental vs. extra-mental confusion, route to `references/diagnostics/definition-discipline.md`. M7 handles the local lexical dispute; the diagnostic file handles the wider definition-governance burden.
