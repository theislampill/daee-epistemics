## What This System Is

daee-epistemics is a **diagnostic runtime** for the restoration of noetic soundness. It is not an argument database. It is not a fatwa engine.
It is not a topic-keyed retrieval system. It is not a debate scorecard.

It is a governed pipeline that takes an arbitrary natural-language input
— any claim, any objection, any worldview assertion, any crisis of faith, any philosophical provocation —
diagnoses the noetic structure of that input, identifies every active deformation, routes each deformation to its licensed restorative operation, executes those operations recursively until all deformations are addressed, and produces a proof-closed output whose graphable structure can be audited by a human or a checker.

The system exists to serve the dāʿī (the caller to Islam, the one who invites toward sound knowledge and sound worship). Every architectural decision is downstream of that purpose.

## The Epistemological Ground

The system is built around the fiṭrah baseline: the innate disposition of every rational agent toward sound knowledge, tawḥīd, and sound worship.
This is not a theological opinion held by the system. It is the system's **architectural axiom**.

What follows from this axiom:

**Soundness is not constructed; it is uncovered.**
The restorative operation does not install new beliefs.
It removes the occlusion that separates the interlocutor from what they already innately know.
This means the endpoint of any collapse proof is not persuasion — it is the removal of what is blocking the fiṭrah signal.

**Every deformation has a source.**
Shubuhāt (epistemic corruptions, doubts weaponized against sound knowledge) do not arise from nothing.
They arise from specific epistemic mechanisms:
imported tribunals, self-authorizing criteria, predicate misapplication, desire-capture of the rational faculty, testimony distortion, source-order inversion, and so on.
Each mechanism has a corresponding restorative operation.
The owner/TTP catalogue is the map of that correspondence.

**The fiṭrah is the restorative ground-state; analogous to a minimum-entropy attractor.**
Every noetic field, however corrupted, is in principle, and is treated by the system as, restorable toward its ground state because the ground state is innate, not installed. In the provisional Shannon framing below, this ground state is modeled as a minimum-entropy attractor.
The collapse proof is a proof that the restorative operations have reduced the noetic entropy (i.e. removed the diagnosed occlusions that block return to that ground) — not by force, but by removing what does not belong.

This is not abstract theology. It is the engineering specification.
It tells the system:

- what the target state is (fiṭrah baseline);
- what the operations must do (remove occlusion, not construct argument);
- what counts as success (deformation addressed by verified operation with licensed Land
  contribution, not interlocutor persuaded);
- what counts as failure (HOLD/PARTIAL with named unresolved burden, not fabricated closure).

### Fiṭrah as the terminus of proof

The fiṭrah is not merely the endpoint toward which restoration moves. It is also the ground from which proof becomes possible.

Discursive reasoning (`naẓar`) cannot justify itself through an endless chain of further discursive premises.
Any proof that produces knowledge must ultimately return to premises known necessarily from the fiṭrah (`muqaddimāt ḍarūriyya fiṭriyya`).
Otherwise the proof collapses into circularity or infinite regress: inferred premises would always need other inferred premises, with no non-inferential ground.

This means the aim of proof is not to manufacture certainty from outside the interlocutor.
The aim of proof is to return the burden to intuitive primordial knowledge initiated by God in the `qalb` — the heart/mind — where necessary knowledge is already grounded.

In daee terms:

- Layer B operations do not construct soundness;
- they trace burdens back to the fiṭrī necessary ground;
- `Land(B)` is licensed only when the burden has been returned to a non-circular, non-regressive ground;
- `HOLD/PARTIAL` is required when the proof has not yet reached that ground;
- fabricated closure is forbidden because it would imitate proof without returning to fiṭrī necessity.

This strengthens the architecture: the runtime is not merely reducing noetic noise.
It is forcing every inferential burden back to the necessary knowledge without which inference itself cannot stand.

## The Central Engineering Problem

**The noetic structure selection will not be known at design time.**

This is not a limitation. It is the central thesis, and it generates the core engineering requirement:
the system must be designed to operate over the entire space of possible noetic structures, not over the subset of cases the designer anticipated.

The stress cases — Trinitarian, TST/Lillard, Boltzmann Brain, Khaybar, secular moral realism, doubt-churn, hujjah-hold — are **smoke tests**, not the design envelope.
If the system only works for named cases, it is an argument database, not a runtime.
An argument database would be simpler to build and far less useful.

What this means in practice:

The runtime cannot select a TTP by pattern-matching the surface text to a known case label.
It must diagnose the **noetic structure** of the input — the actual epistemic machinery at work, independent of which surface argument is being made — and route from structure, not from surface.

"I am a Boltzmann brain" and "the Trinity is the correct understanding of God" and "Islam has no epistemic basis" are different surface arguments.
Each may, when properly diagnosed, activate overlapping noetic families:
self-undermining epistemic structure, imported epistemic tribunal, predicate misapplication, fiṭrah occlusion.
The routing is determined by the diagnosed structure, not by the case label.

This is why the runtime begins with:
`Diagnostic IR is formed internally. Case State is maintained internally.`

And why the Layer A banner exists before any argument is made.
The banner is the system asserting:
"I have diagnosed the noetic structure before acting."

## Meta-Noetic Memetics: The Solution to the Unknown-Structure Problem

**Meta-noetic memetics** is the layer of the pipeline that operates over the *space* of possible noetic structures rather than within any particular one.

"Meta-noetic" means: the system's operations are not about specific beliefs.
They are about the structural patterns by which beliefs are formed, held, distorted, and restored.
These patterns repeat across interlocutors, cultures, historical periods, and argument families.
The pattern is the unit the system operates on.

"Memetics" in this context means: the propagation and transformation of epistemic units — ideas, claims, distortions — through the noetic field.
A shubha is a memetic entity: it propagates through hosts, adapts surface form, but carries a recognizable structural signature.
The system identifies the structural signature, not the surface argument.

The meta-noetic memetics layer does three things in the pipeline:

**1. Structure selection over the candidate N-frame space.**
For any input D₀, the diagnostic phase scans the noetic-structure selection space and selects or holds candidate N frames.
It does not assume the selected N is known in advance.
This is the explicit `general noetic-selection / register-control reread gate` in the runtime:
"do not assume the selected N frame is known at design time."
That instruction is not a caution.
It is the meta-noetic requirement:
the system must scan the space before committing to a route.

**2. Structural routing over the owner/TTP catalogue.**
Once the N frame is selected and the deformation families are diagnosed, the system routes each diagnosed deformation to its matched owner family through structure-to-owner correspondence, not through keyword match.
The catalogue covers: E1-E4, F1-F3, M1-M9, M1-P, R1-R3, V1-V12, heuristics, P1-P7, transmission/testimony owners, predication owners, grief/register owners, source-worldview owners, and family-local proof-method owners.
The system does not print the catalogue. It executes every structurally live owner as a local Layer B submove.

**3. Recursive burden cycling until the deformation graph is closed.**
The MRP (Mid-Reread Pressure) layer generates counter-burdens (𝔅_MRP) from the restorative operations applied to presented burdens (𝔅_LA).
These generated burdens re-enter the pipeline.
The recursion continues until every node in the diagnosed set has a verified activation or an honest HOLD/PARTIAL with the unresolved node named.
This is the finite fold over the burden graph — the mechanism by which an arbitrary noetic input collapses toward soundness through recursive perturbation.

## The Three-Layer Ownership Principle

This is the engineering discipline that keeps the system honest. Every output surface is owned
by exactly one layer. No layer may perform another's function.

| Layer        | Owner                                      | What it does                                                                                                                                             | What it must never do                                                                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Model**    | Fills descriptive activation slots         | Names burden_id, submove_id, owner_id, operation, pressure_label, body_ref, delta_type, delta_result, land_label                                         | Authors notation symbols; authors verification booleans; authors ACT line directly       |
| **Checker**  | Verifies against semantic ground truth     | Dereferences body_ref; confirms pressure in body; confirms operation performed; confirms Δ visible; confirms Land licensed; produces CanonicalActivation | Trusts model-authored claims; accepts self-attestation booleans; accepts ledger as proof |
| **Renderer** | Derives notation from canonical activation | Produces `⟦ACT ⁿBₘ[owner.operation] :: π=... :: body_ref=... :: Δ=... :: Land(ⁿB)+⟧` deterministically                                                   | Accepts model-authored notation; generates notation from prose                           |

**The body is semantic ground truth.** Everything the checker verifies is verified against the
body — not against the model's claim about the body, not against a ledger the model wrote about
the body, not against a boolean the model filled about the body. The body.

**A false or missing checker verification field produces HOLD/PARTIAL or FAIL.** It never
produces a nearby notation. The model does not get to emit a plausible-looking ACT line when the
body doesn't support it. It marks the burden unresolved.

## The Shannon Discipline

*(Provisional engineering framing — not a completed formalism.)*

The system is structurally analogous to a reliable channel decoder. The fiṭrah signal exists in every rational agent.
Shubuhāt are noise. The dāʿī's operation is decoding — recovering the signal from the noise.

Shannon proved that reliable transmission over a noisy channel is possible if and only if you build sufficient redundancy into the encoding.
The activation_record is that redundancy:
body_ref, pressure_label, delta_type, and land_label are independent cross-checks.
A corrupted ACT line cannot pass all of them simultaneously.
Notation drift (Π=, Λ=, +R, DeltaB) is channel corruption — detectable by the redundant structure, not by prose instruction.
Shannon showed that reliable communication over a noisy channel depends on the relation between source entropy, channel capacity, coding, and noise;
in engineering terms, redundancy and constrained encoding make errors detectable and correctable when the system operates within its reliable capacity.

The collapse of the noetic field to soundness is, in this framing, a finite fold over the burden graph:

```
CollapseProof(Φ) = fold over 𝔅_total = 𝔅_LA ∪ 𝔅_MRP
  where each node contributes only if:
    owner/TTP route is catalogue-backed;
    body_ref dereferences;
    operation is performed in the body;
    pressure is targeted in the body;
    Δκ / ΔⁿB is visible;
    Land contribution is licensed;
    field_witness mirrors the activation.
```

Provisional research notation only:

```
Collapse(Φ) ≈ Fold_{B ∈ 𝔅_total}(verified τ(B), Δκ(B), Land(B))
```

Do not claim this as a proven formalism until the repo defines dB, the measure/ordering of Δκ, H(Φ), H_fiṭrah, and convergence checking.
The Shannon analogy clarifies why redundancy, canonicalization, and decoding checks matter. It does not by itself prove noetic collapse.

## Natural Language Autoencoders and Reconstructibility

The graphed proof is the external audit surface for the collapse certificate.
It is not proof by itself; it renders what the checker, Closure/Reconstruction Witness, and field_witness certify.
If the collapse is real:

- Every diagnosed deformation node appears in the graph
- Every arc corresponds to a verified activation
- Every Land node has a licensed contribution
- No unresolved orphan deformation node is hidden; any disconnected, held, partial, or non-load-bearing node is explicitly represented and explained
- The field_witness closes the proof as a graph-completeness certificate, not just a structural parser

The NLA (Natural Language Autoencoder) property is the full encode-decode cycle:

```
natural language body
  → activation_record (descriptive slots filled by model)
  → checker verification (semantic ground truth)
  → CanonicalActivation (checker-derived)
  → canonical ACT line (renderer-derived)
  → graphed node
  → Restorative Response
  → Closing Formulation
  → Closure/Reconstruction Witness
  → field_witness mirror
```

The decode direction — canonical ACT → reconstructed natural language equivalent with semantic equivalence — is a target, not yet implemented.
The foundation is laid by the encode direction.
NLA reconstructibility (Output Grapher `parserVerdict.reconstructible: true`) currently proves that the proof can be parsed and traversed, not that the full encode-decode cycle is closed.

## Progressive Skill Disclosure

The system loads owner bodies progressively, not all at once. This is not merely a performance optimization.
It is the enforcement of the meta-noetic principle: the system must not pre-select an argument before diagnosing the structure.

The always-loaded runtime carries only:

- the diagnostic grammar (Layer A fields, N-frame selection, register activation)
- the routing governance invariants (diagnosis before answer; IR before routing; routing before render)
- the output contract (banner, Layer A/B shape, Land/R/reread cycle, closure witness)
- the ACT/canonicalization rule: model-filled slots are descriptive only; checker-derived CanonicalActivation is authoritative
- the invalid-token rule and HOLD/PARTIAL failure behavior

Owner-specific operation detail loads only after the diagnostic IR has authorized that owner through the owner/TTP entry criteria.
Bundle co-location is not activation.
Loading the bundle is not activation.
Executing the owner body with target → operation → result is activation.

## The Guarantee (Honest Form)

The system can eventually guarantee:

> For any input whose deformations are fully diagnosed by the rubric, and whose deformation
> nodes are all matched to catalogue-backed owner/TTP operations, the runtime will either
> produce a checker-verified, field_witness-mirrored, graphable collapse proof, or
> honestly return HOLD/PARTIAL with unmatched or unresolved nodes named.

The qualifiers are structural, not hedging:

- **Fully diagnosed** — the diagnostic rubric must cover the deformation family present in the
  input. If a new deformation type appears that the rubric cannot name, the system surfaces an
  unmatched node. That is honest behavior, not failure.

- **Catalogue-backed** — the owner catalogue must have a licensed TTP for the matched deformation
  family. If it does not, the system surfaces PARTIAL/OWNER-BODY-NOT-LOADED. That is honest
  behavior, not failure.

The guarantee is not omniscience. It is proof integrity: if the system claims collapse, the collapse is checker-verified; if it cannot verify, it says so.

## What the Agent Must Never Do

**Never treat a passing checker gate as behavioral proof.**
Static gates prove the fixture set.
Fresh smokes prove the runtime.

**Never treat a passing ACT surface syntax check as semantic proof.**
Correct notation does not mean the body performed the operation.

**Never emit a fabricated closure.**
If a burden is unresolved, write PARTIAL and name the burden.
Polished-looking output that conceals an unresolved node is a worse failure than an honest PARTIAL.

**Never trust a model-authored boolean as proof.**
`body_performs_operation: true` written by the model is not evidence that the body performed the operation.
The checker verifies.
The model fills slots.

**Never select a TTP by surface pattern-matching.**
Read the Layer A diagnostic fields before routing.
The N frame is not known at design time.
The diagnostic must run.

**Never expand the forbidden-symbol prose list as the main solution to notation drift.**
Notation drift is an encoding-space problem, not an instruction problem.
The solution is constraining the encoding space, not describing the drift more precisely.

**Never claim total noetic field collapse, graph completeness, NLA reconstructibility, or Shannon-theorem proof from per-activation passing alone.**
These are target layers. Per-activation proof is a necessary precondition, not the whole proof.

**Never package, commit, push, tag, or release while the backlog has open release-blocking rows.**

## When the Agent Gets Lost

If you lose orientation, return to this sequence:

1. The dāʿī serves by removing occlusion from the fiṭrah, not by winning arguments. Every
   technical decision exists to make that service reliable, honest, and provable.

2. The input noetic structure is unknown at design time. The system must work for all structures,
   not just named cases. Any patch that only fixes a named case without generalizing is a
   suppression, not a fix.

3. The body is semantic ground truth. If you are unsure whether an activation is valid, ask:
   "Does the dereferenced body perform the owner operation against the named pressure and produce
   a visible Δ?" If no, the activation is not valid regardless of what the model wrote.

4. The checker canonicalizes. The model fills slots. The renderer produces notation. If these
   three things are confused in the implementation you are reading, that is the problem to fix.

5. HOLD/PARTIAL is an honest answer. Fabricated closure is not. The system's integrity depends
   on its willingness to say "I could not fully resolve this" when it cannot.

6. The next smallest safe action is in the orchestrator document. Read it before acting.
