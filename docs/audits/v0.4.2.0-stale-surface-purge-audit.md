# v0.4.2.0 Stale-Surface / Old-Tools / Old-Smokes Purge Audit

Boundary: this audit was run after the RC2 implementation-completeness pass and before packaging a
pre-public Deep Research archive. It does not publish, tag, push, or create a GitHub Release. It
preserves the RC2 proof boundary: source/build/static checker evidence is stronger than prompt
engineering, but public release provenance proof, current-release smoke proof, live-output
behavioral proof, model-host generalization proof, and full noetic-field-calculus proof remain
absent.

## Summary

- Generated docs were fresh: `tools/build_docs_index.py --check` and
  `tools/check_docs_index_interactions.py` passed.
- No tracked tool was deleted. Several older tools are retained as compatibility aliases,
  workflow-required gates, manual developer tools, or research/live-smoke helpers.
- The tracked current-release smoke skeleton under `tests/smokes/current-release/v0.4.2.0/`
  remains pending-only and is not smoke proof.
- Ignored raw fixture candidates remain ignored and untracked under `_fixture-candidates/`; they
  must be excluded from the Deep Research repo archive.
- Ignored stale local output surfaces were cleaned where they posed archive-confusion risk:
  old RC1 archive staging/output under `build/` and ignored `tests/output/`.
- Ignored historical local smoke roots under `smokes/` and `level3-runs/` were not deleted because
  audit history references those paths as historical evidence. They remain ignored and must be
  excluded from the Deep Research repo archive.
