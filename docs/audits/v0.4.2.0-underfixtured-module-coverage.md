## Proof Boundary

Direct fixtures close the static routing-fixture gap from the operativity audit. They do not prove
route correctness under paraphrase, live host behavior, behavioral generalization, or interlocutor
uptake. Future smoke/live work should use blind paraphrase probes, false-route minimal pairs, held
route checks, and closure/reread scoring without exact expected model answers.
