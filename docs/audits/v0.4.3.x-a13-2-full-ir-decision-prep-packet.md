## Why A.13.2 Matters

A.13.2 matters because the current hard-register object is accepted and
guarded, but not yet broadly reconciled with every register-bearing proof
surface. That leaves full B.5 IR decode and hard-register A.10/A.12
isomorphism blocked by the gap between "hard registers validate as an IR
object" and "hard registers are the canonical comparison state for field
witness, NAR, and mixed-register composition."

This would help D.3/register-composition/mixed-concealment only indirectly. D.3
AS/MM static slices are already source-boundary recorded, and fixture 62 remains
a separate D.3-adjacent owner decision. The A.13-adjacent routing fixture is
`tests/routing-fixtures/63-register-composition-owner-handoff.json`: it names a
mixed live set including `heart`, `xi`, `Omega`, `mu`, `kappa`, and `sigma`.
The A.13.2 value is to prove the five hard registers stay composed without
folding `sigma` into the hard-register object or collapsing mixed pressure into
one owner route.

## Recommended First Slice

Recommend `A.13.2 - hard-register field_witness/NAR/register-composition
reconciliation`.

This slice should be:

- version-gated behind `0.4.3-hard-registers-v1`;
- backward-compatible with all schema-light outputs and retained proof corpus
  artifacts;
- checker/fixture-first, with no runtime/default emission;
- narrow enough to review as a hard-register consistency gate;
- explicit that `sigma` remains source-status/source-basis evidence and is not
  added to the hard-register object;
- explicit that full IR decode remains a later B.5/A.13 follow-on.

Expected implementation shape if accepted:

1. Add hard-register reconciliation checks that compare:
   - `registers` live/held keys;
   - `field_witness.normalized_activation_record.live_registers`;
   - `field_witness.register_deltas` or equivalent register-delta evidence when
     present;
   - `coverage_proof.diagnostic_completeness.live_registers` or equivalent
     coverage mapping when present.
2. Add a positive mixed-register composition fixture derived from the fixture 63
   pressure family without changing fixture 62 or treating D.3 as accepted.
3. Add invalid canaries for collapsed/missing/mis-keyed register composition.
4. Keep `tools/check_nla_decode_semantic_faithfulness.py` bounded; do not turn
   it into full IR decode.

## Risks

- Forcing hard registers too early could make schema-light retained outputs fail
  despite the explicit A.13.1 compatibility boundary.
- Runtime/default emission could bloat governed outputs and encourage users to
  read internal IR as public prose.
- Full IR decode could be overclaimed if the work only proves ACT/body
  reconstruction or hard-register live-set comparison.
- Register composition could be damaged if mixed pressure is flattened into a
  single route or if `sigma` is mistakenly added as a sixth hard-register key.
- Hard-register fields could be confused with interlocutor uptake; they are
  runtime-side diagnostic/control state only.
- A.13.2 could accidentally reopen fixture 62/D.3 if fixture 63 composition is
  not kept separate from the D.3-adjacent unknown-pattern lane.
