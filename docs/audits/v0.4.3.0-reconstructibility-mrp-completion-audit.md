## MRP

Verified:

- `TTP-MRP-mid-reread-pressure.md` is a real tactic/operator contract;
- it is catalogue-registered and included in compiled runtime;
- it remains a reread-time activation harness, not a replacement for hidden-framework, entailment,
  doubt-churn, or reorientation owners;
- six pressure slots are required;
- every pressure slot must name owner/TTP, pressure class, or coverage gap;
- pre-emptive downstream pressure is graph/commitment/framework-bound and dispatches to existing
  matched TTPs;
- MRP can contract fake burdens, HOLD partial real burdens, RECURSE into genuine downstream
  burdens, and pre-empt a structurally licensed defense.

## R(H,Δ) operational consequence

Verified: MRP and its checker fail cosmetic reread where `R(H,Δ)` appears without observable
reread consequence. Acceptable consequences include no-new-burden/stability, graph edge, HOLD,
recoil mark, LoopBreak, reorientation, closure witness update, or `field_witness` update.

## ∇·T / ∇×T active gates

- non-null `∇×T` requires LoopBreak, STOP, HOLD, or graph-bound recursion explanation;
- churn plus graph expansion fails;
- proof-stacking after LoopBreak fails.
