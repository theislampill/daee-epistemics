## Purpose

This document owns the repair-and-rerun law for the five governed DAEE smokes. It
does not own the theological answers, prescribe burden or submove counts, or turn
model output length into a pass condition.

The governing objective is:

> One immutable package-faithful candidate must pass all five registered inputs
> through Stage01-Stage08, cold independent review, and human review in one
> cycle. Any registered ANDON stops that cycle, triggers IMPLEMENTAUDIT repair
> discipline, and requires a new candidate plus a complete five-case rerun.

Historical v0.4.5.0 release-repair evidence in
`docs/audits/v0.4.5.0-release-repair-replacement.md` demonstrates the predecessor
pattern: all cases were observed before classification, failures were preserved,
countermeasures were committed and pushed, and a fresh all-at-once matrix followed.
This North Star generalizes that pattern from four to five inputs and makes the
loop a prospective contract rather than a historical accident.

## Candidate and Cycle Identity

A candidate is an immutable package, not a mutable directory or branch label.
Its record binds:

- exact 40-hex source commit;
- clean-tree digest;
- generated-runtime and build-manifest hashes;
- package profile;
- archive hash;
- extracted-tree hash;
- candidate-build authorization hash;
- candidate ID and custody root;
- single-use state `READY_UNUSED`, `CONSUMED_OBSERVED`,
  `CONSUMED_NO_DISPATCH`, `CONSUMED_DISPATCH_UNKNOWN`, or `QUARANTINED`.

Candidate-build authorization binds the candidate ID and unused output root at
issuance and is atomically consumed before filesystem mutation. Replaying an
authorization or moving a consumed/quarantined candidate back to `READY_UNUSED`
is forbidden.

Candidate custody records stochastic use, not quality. A failure before the
atomic cycle claim may leave the candidate `READY_UNUSED`. After claim, at
least one proved producer dispatch yields `CONSUMED_OBSERVED`; proved zero
dispatch yields `CONSUMED_NO_DISPATCH`; and indeterminate dispatch yields
terminal `CONSUMED_DISPATCH_UNKNOWN`. No post-claim state returns to ready, and
unknown dispatch blocks further paid use until reconciled. Structural replay
later owns `structural-pre-review-verdict`, and cold/human adjudication later
owns the final `cycle-verdict`. Candidate state MUST NOT say PASS or FAIL.

A matrix cycle binds:

- one `READY_UNUSED` candidate record and hash;
- the five-case registry and hash;
- canonical package-faithful entrypoint `tools/run_five_smoke_matrix.py` and
  runner adapter `codex`;
- exact producer model (`gpt-5.5`), reasoning setting (`high`), resolved model
  identifier, Codex adapter version, and host/application version;
- context, tool, output, continuation, and transport policy;
- five isolated run directories;
- one cycle ID and launch window;
- campaign/cycle authorization and hash;
- an external create-if-absent cycle-claim receipt, unused working root, and
  claim-bound fallback observation-finalizer path;
- expected canonical campaign-usage head hash/sequence;
- unique external staging, final, and publish-pointer paths;
- predecessor cycle-verdict hash and increasing campaign ordinal;
- evidence-retention policy and exact retention/readback method.

The owner-selected canonical producer lane is package-faithful
`tools/run_five_smoke_matrix.py --model-runner codex` with `gpt-5.5` and
reasoning effort `high`. The authorization and launch record MUST retain the
exact resolved model identifier, Codex adapter version, and host/application
version actually used. Changing entrypoint, adapter, model, or reasoning creates
a new protocol and cannot occur inside a cycle. A normal Codex Desktop task or
direct staged-runner invocation is not the canonical completion lane.

Standing campaign authorization binds the uncapped convergence objective,
branch, registry, package profile, producer/reviewer model families, reasoning
settings, evidence retention, and validity/revocation state. It does not encode
a fixed cycle ceiling or cumulative invocation give-up rule. The coordinator
mints exact one-use child reservations: five producer calls for a GPT cycle and,

reviews. Usage and cost are always recorded factually. Conflicts, orphaned or
unknown usage, open ANDONs, failed cold review, and provider circuit breakers

a green row in another cycle.

## No-Model Candidate Maturity Gate

The full five-model cycle is an expensive behavioral integration gate, not the
primary debugger. Branch-9 deterministic infrastructure exists to move failure
discovery left. A candidate is ineligible for model execution until the
hash-bound no-model verdict is:

```text
NO_MODEL_CANDIDATE_MATURE
```

That verdict requires, at the same source commit and generated/package state:

1. every known open ANDON is closed, deferred with an explicit model-safe basis,
   or blocks launch;
2. every prior model-smoke defect that is deterministically reproducible has a
   retained negative canary and neighboring valid control;
3. each countermeasure canary is proven red at the pre-fix boundary and green
   at the candidate boundary for the intended reason;
4. A11 expectation-sidecar and mutation/right-reason checks pass;
5. Stage01-Stage08 valid/invalid handshake fixtures pass at their rightful
   earliest stage and forbid downstream artifacts after failure;
6. A15 topology-capacity and metamorphic probes cover low/high cardinality,
   split/merge, lifecycle, recursion, and projection without topic answers;
7. A10 cross-stage parity, A12 load-path delivery, A13 package/harness parity,
   generated-runtime freshness, package shape, and docs/claim gates pass;
8. captured historical failures replay against the current validation registry;
9. no checker is satisfied by case name, copied output phrase, fixed byte floor,
   fixed burden count, fixed submove count, or expected theological conclusion;
10. fake-Codex-adapter barrier, campaign usage-head conflict/orphan recovery,
    cycle-claim/root-fallback, and staged/atomic evidence-publish fixtures pass
    without model calls;
11. the maturity verdict binds the exact source commit, clean-tree digest,
    registry hash, checker-registry hash, generated-runtime hash, candidate
    package record, commands, exits, and artifact hashes.

`PREFLIGHT_GREEN_AWAITING_OWNER_AUTHORIZATION` remains a non-authorizing
readiness token only when no valid standing campaign can mint the required
child. Under the active owner-issued standing campaign, exact-SHA CI green plus
`NO_MODEL_CANDIDATE_MATURE` permits the coordinator to mint the next exact
one-use producer/review child without another design pause. The maturity token
alone never authorizes spend, Git, release packaging, or publication.

Source-owned deterministic checks first emit
`NO_MODEL_SOURCE_PREFLIGHT_GREEN` for the exact pushed SHA. That token may
authorize candidate packaging, but it cannot authorize model execution. Final
`NO_MODEL_CANDIDATE_MATURE` is issued only after one immutable `READY_UNUSED`
candidate exists and the source preflight, exact-SHA CI receipt, package/load
path, escape registry, and deterministic reports are joined to that candidate.

The owner reports that predecessor four-smoke repair campaigns sometimes needed
dozens of full model invocations to expose and repair small defects. That history
is a P0 process abnormality, not merely a budget inconvenience. It means the
deterministic gate failed to make known structural, lifecycle, projection,
custody, or checker-contract defects cheap and reproducible before paid
integration. The target operating model is therefore:

```text
cheap deterministic discovery and localization
  -> exact-SHA candidate maturity
  -> one expensive five-case behavioral observation
  -> independent review
```

It MUST NOT be inverted into repeated model calls followed by retrospective
fixture writing. A model cycle is justified only for residual behavior that the
current deterministic contract cannot establish. Cost control never weakens the
five-case completion rule; it strengthens the evidence required before spending
the five calls.

## Model-Smoke Escape Accounting

Any defect first discovered by a paid/model smoke creates a
`MODEL_SMOKE_ESCAPE` process row in addition to the product ANDON. The row asks:

```yaml
escape_id: immutable id
cycle_id: failed cycle
product_andon_ids: [actual ids]
first_observed_stage_or_review: exact gate
deterministic_detectability: YES | NO | UNKNOWN
why_preflight_did_not_detect: causal evidence
missing_or_failed_canary_owner: exact source/checker/fixture owner
countermeasure: exact action
red_boundary_evidence: command, commit, and result
green_boundary_evidence: command, commit, and result
mutation_or_right_reason_evidence: command and result
next_model_cycle_eligible: false
recurrence_of_escape_id: null-or-prior-id
model_invocations_spent_before_detection: integer
estimated_model_invocations_avoided_by_canary: integer-or-unknown
```

- `YES`: the missing deterministic control is a process ANDON. Add and prove it
  before another model cycle.
- `UNKNOWN`: investigate until classified; another paid cycle is blocked.
- `NO`: document why the defect depends on genuinely probabilistic/semantic
  behavior that deterministic fixtures cannot reproduce. Strengthen review or
  observability where possible without inventing an answer bank.

A `NO` classification is scoped to an exact source, schema, checker,
model-protocol, defect signature, and inspected artifact boundary. It names the
missing topic-neutral observable, anti-answer-bank reason, strongest available
observability, accountable owner, and recheck predicates. Automated recurrence
or pattern analysis may append `REASSESSMENT_DUE`; it may not mutate `NO` to
`UNKNOWN`. That transition requires a hash-bound accountable-owner plus
independent-reviewer adjudication identifying materially new evidence. A named
`UNKNOWN` blocks paid execution until it becomes `YES` with a valid canary or a
renewed scoped `NO`. Another cycle under a legitimate `NO` still requires 5
Whys, Hansei, a credible owner-source countermeasure, strongest neutral
observability, deterministic green, and independent concurrence.

The objective is not to prove that models are unnecessary. It is to reserve
model calls for behavior that cannot be established by deterministic contracts
and to prevent repeated paid discovery of small structural errors. Track model
invocations and escape classes per campaign so Hansei can show whether
deterministic coverage is actually improving.

The campaign summary MUST report, without claiming false precision:

- total producer and reviewer invocations attempted, completed, cancelled, and
  not dispatched;
- model cycles blocked by deterministic maturity failures;
- model-smoke escapes by deterministic-detectability class and owner surface;
- repeated escapes that an existing canary should have caught;
- permanent canaries added and their red/green/right-reason evidence;
- model invocations avoided only where a planned cycle was actually blocked by
  a deterministic failure, otherwise `unknown`;
- factual producer/reviewer usage and cost, including unresolved or unknown
  reservations.

The desired trend is fewer deterministic escapes and fewer paid calls per
accepted candidate, not fewer or thinner model observations. A falling call
count is not success if review quality, topology fidelity, or evidence custody
falls with it.

## Parallel Observation and Usage Contract

All five cases MUST launch concurrently through isolated workers after one
preflight and one immutable launch snapshot. The machine-verifiable protocol is
`barrier-five-submit-before-await-v1`; `parallelism=5` by itself is not proof.
Each worker receives one case, one fresh context, one read-only package copy or
verified immutable package root, one private temp/home/cache root, one private
API/session context, and one new run directory.

Parallel workers MUST NOT share:

- conversation history;
- mutable run directories;
- generated state capsules except through the case's own staged chain;
- repair prompts or findings from another case;
- post-launch source, package, or checker mutations.

The hash-chained dispatch manifest MUST show all five `worker_ready` events
before one barrier release, followed by five provider acceptance or Codex
adapter `call_entered_in_flight` acknowledgments before any result is awaited,
observed, parsed, checked, or acted on. The fifth acknowledgment precedes the
first terminal-result event. This is a causal-order proof, not an elapsed-time
threshold.

The coordinator runs a no-output provider/auth/rate-limit and fake-runner
capacity probe before paid launch, then waits for complete observation instead
of failing fast on a case-local product failure. If five accepted/in-flight
isolated starts cannot be proved, launch stops as `PARALLEL_CAPACITY_BLOCKED`; a
queued/sequential batch or await-before-fifth-submit trace is not relabeled
parallel evidence. A shared provider/auth/rate-limit failure trips a circuit
breaker, cancels calls not yet dispatched where the host permits, and marks
remaining rows `NOT_RUN_INFRASTRUCTURE`. It never masquerades as five independent
model failures.

Immediately before dispatch, the coordinator reserves exactly five producer
invocations by advancing one canonical content-addressed campaign usage head
through an exclusive writer or compare-and-swap transaction. Producer and cold
review lanes share that head. If two child authorizations name the same
predecessor, exactly one wins; the loser records `USAGE_HEAD_CONFLICT` and
dispatches zero calls. The always-run observation finalizer settles attempted,
completed, failed after dispatch, cancelled, and not-dispatched counts through a
second head transaction. An orphaned reservation is retained and recovered
conservatively; unknown usage blocks more calls and is never returned as free
capacity. Unused reserved slots are evidence, not same-candidate retry credits.

## ANDON Registration

Any of these conditions registers an ANDON and prevents cycle completion:

- a Stage01-Stage08 failure, partial, truncation, refusal, missing artifact, or
  failure to execute the expected stage;
- producer outcome `NOT_RUN_POLICY_INCOMPATIBILITY`, `PRODUCT_REFUSAL`, or
  `REFUSAL_ORIGIN_UNPROVEN`, with candidate consumption derived separately from
  claim/dispatch evidence;
- source, branch, package, registry, model, host, settings, or authorization
  drift;
- canonical entrypoint/`codex` adapter drift or missing exact resolved version
  capture;
- shared-state or run-directory contamination;
- usage-head predecessor conflict, nonmonotonic head, or orphan treated as free
  capacity;
- fewer than five accepted/in-flight requests before first-result observation;
- missing cycle claim/fallback or unfinalized root-creation failure;
- partial/hash-conflicted evidence staging offered as final;
- semantic normalization or repair of raw model output;
- a structural checker failing for an unregistered or wrong reason;
- a cold-review challenge that the human independently upholds or cannot
  resolve, concerning a material omitted pressure, collapsed burden,
  underdeveloped operation, false resultant, false recursion, or
  unreconstructible closure;
- a human reviewer finding a material theological, factual, source,
  argumentative, topology, body-depth, or comprehensiveness defect;
- unresolved disagreement between review lanes;
- a completion claim assembled from more than one candidate or cycle.

Infrastructure failure is not silently relabeled model failure. It still fails
the cycle, is preserved, and receives its own failure class. A replacement
cycle begins only after the infrastructure cause and evidence boundary are
understood. A proved external-only repair needs no fake repository commit; when
no repository or candidate-bearing boundary changed, it may use the same exact
SHA with a fresh candidate/cycle identity after no-model health is restored.

The coordinator first writes a create-if-absent cycle-claim receipt outside the
working root. If root creation fails, the claim-bound fallback path still
receives `kind: cycle-observation-finalizer`, candidate
`CONSUMED_NO_DISPATCH` only when zero dispatch is proved, otherwise
`CONSUMED_OBSERVED` or `CONSUMED_DISPATCH_UNKNOWN`, and preserved failure
evidence.

The neutral observation finalizer MUST run after every launch outcome, including
nonzero runner exit, cancellation, infrastructure stop, missing row, or root
failure. It writes invocation/usage-head settlement or orphan status, neutral
candidate-consumption state, per-case terminal status, first failures, barrier
events, and artifact inventory. It MUST NOT emit the reviewed cycle verdict.
Structural replay later emits `structural-pre-review-verdict`; only the complete
cold/human review join emits `cycle-verdict`.

The exporter writes the complete success/failure/partial cycle or fallback
bundle to the authorization-bound unique staging path, writes and reads back the
retention manifest, then atomically publishes an unused final path or
CAS-advances an immutable object-store pointer. Resume is allowed only for
hash-equal files bound to the same cycle claim/export lineage. Partial staging,
hash mismatch, or missing final readback keeps the cycle `PARTIAL` and never
deletes or repairs raw capture. Ignored `.daee/` working custody alone cannot
satisfy completion.

The canonical external root is the absolute `evidence_custody_root` bound into
the one-use campaign authorization. Its owner-specific resolved value remains
in private authorization/evidence rather than tracked source. The authorized
root has create-if-absent `.staging`, `claims`, `cycles`, and `pointers`
children. Every raw cycle is retained indefinitely by hash. Any later pruning
is separately owner-authorized and leaves permanent manifest, provenance,
removed-hash, and removal-reason residue.

## Countermeasure and Canary Law

A countermeasure is eligible for a new model cycle only when:

1. it patches the canonical owner/source rather than a generated file or final
   answer;
2. a retained negative canary fails on the pre-fix boundary for the intended
   reason;
3. the same canary passes after the fix;
4. neighboring valid behavior remains valid;
5. the canary detects a transition, relation, or custody violation rather than
   a copied phrase;
6. no case-name or Torah/Qur'an-specific gate is introduced;
7. Smoke A and Smoke B pass at the same source state;
8. generated runtime, docs, package shape, and deterministic CI are fresh where
   affected.
9. any associated `MODEL_SMOKE_ESCAPE` row has terminal deterministic-
   detectability classification and, when reproducible, a proven permanent
   canary.

Counts such as 10/20 burdens or 3/6/8 submoves are capacity probes only. They
are never universal output requirements.

## Recurrence and Escalation

“Loop until all five pass” does not mean retry until stochastic success. There
is no arbitrary numeric retry cap, but every new model cycle requires a new
causal basis and changed, verified candidate.

When the same failure class recurs:

- stop another model cycle;
- compare all prior causal records and canaries;
- inspect whether the prior countermeasure patched a symptom, harness-only
  instruction, or checker tolerance;
- escalate to the owning architecture, schema, load path, producer/checker
  contract, or lifecycle reducer;
- add a recurrence canary that distinguishes the deeper root from the prior
  symptom;
- resume only after the deeper deterministic gate is green.

## Cold GPT-5.6 Review Contract

The cold reviewer MUST have no conversation history or informal project
familiarity. “Cold” does not mean context-starved. It receives a self-contained,
hash-bound packet containing:

- exact smoke input;
- unmodified candidate output;
- a concise statement of what DAEE output is meant to accomplish;
- the review rubric and non-claims;
- Stage01-Stage08 records;
- public witness, audit envelope, and relevant body references;
- no expected answer, burden map, submove map, source list, or conclusion.

Before grading, it reconstructs:

1. the candidate's central thesis and intended response;
2. the material input pressures it believes were selected;
3. the burden and submove topology actually present;
4. each performed operation and claimed local/aggregate resultant;
5. generated, held, pre-empted, and unresolved states;
6. the restorative and closure trajectory.

The review artifact separately records:

- `comprehension_status: PASS | REVIEW_INVALID`;
- `coverage_verdict: PASS | PARTIAL | FAIL | NOT_GRADED`;
- challenged source-unit, pressure, burden, operation, resultant, and witness
  IDs;
- evidence and recommended disposition for each challenge;
- an explicit statement that output length and formal notation presence were
  not used as substitutes for execution.

A reviewer that cannot demonstrate basic comprehension produces
`REVIEW_INVALID`, not an automatic candidate PASS or FAIL. The human reviewer
then classifies whether the cause is reviewer/transport failure or candidate
intelligibility. Candidate-intelligibility failure registers an ANDON.

Every failed or ambiguous cold-review attempt pauses the campaign and produces
an owner incident report before retry, packet repair, or a successor cycle. The
report binds raw-attempt, reviewer-output, input, producer-output, and packet
hashes; failure class; whether substantive grading occurred; complete lineage;
and the proposed next action. Transport retry reuses a valid packet hash.
Packet-construction repair creates a new packet hash only with predecessor,
exact delta, deterministic red/green builder evidence, unchanged input/output
proof, and anti-answer-bank evidence. A shared review-contract change repeats
all five reviews in the cohort. No attempt is selected by favorable verdict.

## Human Review and Adjudication

The human review is independent evidence, not a rubber stamp on either the
model or structural checker. It evaluates:

- material-pressure and topology completeness;
- burden and submove depth relative to the actual input;
- theological, factual, source, and argumentative adequacy;
- whether operations actually license their resultants;
- whether restoration and closure answer the diagnosed field;
- whether the candidate is comprehensive without relying on padding.

One human first writes and hash-claims five immutable independent initial
assessments before any cold review is disclosed. The final review binds those
unchanged hashes and the disclosure receipt. The human may then act as
owner-adjudicator for a disputed cold-model finding. Cold finding IDs and human
adjudications MUST be exact-set equal; each receives `upheld`, `answered`, or
`unresolved`. `answered` requires rationale plus hash-valid evidence bound to
the challenged target IDs. The human cannot waive missing structural evidence,
rewrite the raw output, omit/duplicate a cold finding, or call an unresolved
material challenge PASS.

Cold-model findings begin as preserved challenges, not automatically registered
product ANDONs. An `upheld` or `unresolved` material challenge registers the
ANDON and fails the cycle; an `answered` challenge remains immutable dissent
with the new evidence and rationale that answered it. When the human
adjudicator is also the patch owner, seeks to overturn a material FAIL, or an
unresolved challenge remains, a second independent human/accountable reviewer
is required. Owner-only
`FAIL -> answered -> PASS` without new evidence and a hash-bound affirming
second review remains `PARTIAL`. A second human is otherwise not required.
`REVIEW_INVALID` also records one explicit cause
(`reviewer_transport`, `packet_insufficiency`, or
`candidate_intelligibility`) and predecessor-attempt lineage; only the first two
permit a same-output review retry.
