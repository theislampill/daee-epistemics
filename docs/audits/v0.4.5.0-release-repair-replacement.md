### Phase 3 Hansei / 5 Whys

1. Why did the matrix fail? Three cases did not pass required visible-output validators.
2. Why did TST/Khaybar fail? `check_mrp_route_invariants.py` treated any
   sidecar/NAR `mrp_route_result_type=no_new_resultant` before a later ACT as
   terminal STOP, without distinguishing same-burden submove mirrors from later
   burden traversal.
3. Why did Secularism fail? `normalize_stage05_initial_burden_continuations()`
   rewrote intermediate STOP only when `finding == stable`. The secularism B2 row
   used `finding=hidden-framework-recoil` while still claiming
   `route_result_type=no_new_resultant`, `route=STOP`, `graph_delta=none`, and
   `preemption_basis=none` before later B3 work.
4. Why was this allowed through assembly? The continuation normalizer was keyed to
   one finding token instead of the route-state contradiction itself.
5. Why is the countermeasure sufficient? The route checker now flags only later
   different-burden ACT work for sidecar premature no_new_resultant, while the
   Stage 05 normalizer rewrites any closed intermediate baseline STOP/no_new row
   into `held_burden_activation` for the next already-held burden, independent of
   the finding label.
