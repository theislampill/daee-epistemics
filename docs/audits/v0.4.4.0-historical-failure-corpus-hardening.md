## Andon

ID:
`GATE45_HISTORICAL_FAILURE_CORPUS_CANARY_GAP`

Symptom:
Fresh Stage 07/Stage 08 history still contained expensive-smoke failures whose
exact producer/checker shapes were not represented as focused no-model canaries
before the four-smoke matrix.

Failed check:
Historical corpus mining over `.daee` staged-handoff failures plus
Markdown/ActiveGraph Andon ledgers.

Owner surface:
`tools/run_staged_current_skill_smoke.py`,
`tools/check_staged_runtime_handshake.py` fixtures,
`tools/check_mrp_generated_burden.py` fixtures, and audit docs.

5 Whys:

1. The symptom occurred because Gate 44 exposed Stage 04 `act_row_details`
   mirror drift and Stage 07 ACT delta/owner-mass drift only after full smoke
   execution.
2. That condition existed because earlier canaries covered broad handoff and
   generated-MRP contracts, but not the exact missing mirror-field and
   baseline/held owner-mass producer shapes.
3. The system allowed it because the runner preserved partially populated
   detail objects without hydrating fields already parseable from canonical ACT
   rows, and the negative corpus did not name the newest expensive-smoke
   failure dialects.
4. It was not caught earlier because historical failure families were handled
   lane-by-lane instead of mined as a consolidated no-model failure corpus
   before the next matrix.
5. Recurrence is prevented by adding focused negative fixtures, hydrating only
   missing Stage 04 mirror fields from canonical ACT rows, fixing the ACT delta
   parser dialect asymmetry, and marking remaining case-specific owner-mass
   risk before another matrix.

Hansei:
A passing validator stack is not enough if the corpus lacks the precise failure
dialects that recent smokes actually produced. Historical failures must feed
narrow canaries before the next expensive smoke pass.

Countermeasure:
Patch the runner hydration owner surface, add exact no-model negative fixtures
for unhydrated `act_row_details` and ACT delta/owner-mass drift, repair the
ASCII `ΔB1`/`ΔB5` parser-stability asymmetry in the MRP checker, add generic
SOURCE owner-operation scaffold guidance, and publish this coverage map.
