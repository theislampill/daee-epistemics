Architecture Thesis

What it is

A governed diagnostic runtime.
Surface discourse D₀ is read into a diagnosed noetic/runtime state Ψᴺ,
compact Diagnostic State / IR,
and live registers before public release.
The system asks:
what burden is live,
what owner can act on it, and
what may be released after reread?

What carries weight

Not topic resemblance alone.
Weight is carried by diagnostic state, IR,
route precedence, owner selection,
selected burden ⁿB, owner/TTP operation,
Land(ⁿB), Δ state change, ∇ route pressure,
∇·T / ∇×T diagnostics, R(H,Δ) reread, and
STOP / HOLD / RECURSE
/ PARTIAL / COMPLETE discipline.

What it is not

Not an answer bank, school-label router, proof ledger, public persuasion guarantee, truth meter,
or full formal calculus.
𝒞(Ψᴺ) is a runtime closure condition.
T_lang is a public language-release boundary
toward a diagnosed interlocutor field Ψᴵ,
not guaranteed uptake.

NLA boundary:
NLA means Natural Language Autoencoder:
a reconstruction-fidelity frame for encoding a natural-language discourse state into a controlled intermediate representation and reconstructing it
while preserving burdens, registers, source-status, and dependency structure.
The AV / activation verbalizer analogue is Layer A / Diagnostic IR / noetic-field banner.
The AR / activation reconstructor analogue is reconstruction/reread through R(H,Δ).
Reconstruction fidelity asks whether selected/held N, live registers, burdens, owner/TTP eligibility, κ/H, and closure state can be recovered;
confabulation, excessive expressivity, lack of grounding, degenerate bottleneck, or reconstruction failure forces PARTIAL/RECURSE or checker failure.

Delta and field diagnostics:
Delta-nB and Delta-kappa are event-local transition operators.
∇· / divergence and ∇× / curl are field diagnostics over Delta-produced burden/dependency state.
Default output may use compact governance markers such as ∇·κ: positive/live or ∇×κ:
unresolved loop only when tied to dependency pressure, loop-breaking, R(H,Δ), or PARTIAL/RECURSE/COMPLETE;
long formalism exposition remains audit/formalism-only.

Gradient, closure, and coupling:
plain ∇ reads route-gradient pressure among eligible live routes after gate/catalogue constraints;
LoopBreak(∇×T) is a licensed loop-breaking submove, not arbitrary assertion;
𝒞(Ψᴺ) is positive closure-field condition over the agent execution field;
T_lang: Ψᴺ ⇢ Ψᴵ names the public/restorative output boundary without claiming guaranteed uptake.
