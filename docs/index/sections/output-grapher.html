How to read this

Read top-to-bottom: claim, problems, answer moves, what landed, what remains, follow-up pressure-check, next step, and final restoration.

Technical labels: Land(¹B) means what was established, R(H,Δ) means what remains after that answer, and MRP(¹B) marks the follow-up pressure-check.
