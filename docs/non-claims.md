# Non-Claims and Safety Boundaries

This file is the source-of-truth list for limits that must travel with runtime, docs, release, and audit claims.

- No guaranteed uptake: `T_lang` is a runtime-side coupling attempt. It never proves persuasion, concession, or interlocutor truth-recognition.
- No interior-state certification: concealment modes diagnose discourse-side noetic coverings from evidence. They are not soul-state, sincerity, takfir, kufr, or nifaq-as-fact verdicts.
- No arbitrary-input correctness claim: retained smokes and fixtures prove their scoped evidence rows, not universal behavior.
- No universal semantic grader: current validators are structural, lexical, graph, sidecar, or replay gates unless a future grader explicitly says otherwise.
- No cross-host/paraphrase proof unless a named host/paraphrase matrix was actually run and retained.
- No full formal calculus claim: Shannon/vector/formalism language is provisional engineering framing unless the repo defines and proves the relevant measures.
- No adversarial memetic design: the memetic layer decomposes carriers; it never constructs, optimizes, hardens, or propagates deformation-inducing content.
- No manipulation: diagnosed registers, grief states, profiles, and deformations license matched restorative operations only. They must not be used to engineer assent, target non-epistemic vulnerability, or optimize compliance independent of warrant.

These boundaries are enforceable only to the extent named checkers and fixtures cover the phrasing or structure. They make violations legible; they do not prove manipulation-proofness or semantic impossibility.
