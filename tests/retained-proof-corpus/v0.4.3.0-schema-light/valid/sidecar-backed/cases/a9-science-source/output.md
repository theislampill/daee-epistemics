### ¹B₁[source-status-repair] — bound the source function of science
Target: scientific-explanations-only-knowledge-source: the claim that only scientific explanations count as knowledge.
Operation: source-status-repair.source-order: sort source authority, separate empirical measurement from total epistemic authority, and block hidden support.
Result/state-change: science source bounded; science remains a valid method for measurable phenomena, but it is blocked from silently becoming the judge of all knowledge.
Contribution-to-Land(¹B): this lands ¹B because the scientific method is restored to its proper source function instead of being allowed to decide, without proof, that only its own domain is real.
TTP Operation Body:
Science is powerful where its object is measurable, repeatable, and empirically testable. That gives it real authority over physical regularities, medicine, engineering, observation, and many causal explanations. But the statement "only what science can measure counts as knowledge" is not itself a scientific measurement. It is a philosophical rule about what sources are allowed to count.

So the source-order repair separates method from total tribunal. Islam does not ask you to abandon reason; it refuses to let one limited method swallow reason. Sound reason can recognize mathematical truths, logical laws, moral obligations, testimony, memory, first-person awareness, and metaphysical entailments without pretending each of those is a laboratory object. The delta is that science is bounded as a valid source inside its domain, while the science-only veto is no longer allowed to govern every answer.

### ¹B₂[M1] — test the only-science standard against itself
Target: only-science-counts-standard: the rule that something should not get attention or count as a real answer unless science can measure it.
Operation: M1.self-grounding-test: test whether the claim's own standard can authorize its own verdict.
Result/state-change: self-authorizing standard invalidated; the criterion cannot pass the test it imposes on everything else.
Contribution-to-Land(¹B): this lands ¹B because the exclusion rule defeats itself as a universal knowledge rule, so the Islamic answer may proceed through sound reason without surrendering rationality.
TTP Operation Body:
Apply the rule to itself: can the claim "only scientifically measurable things count as knowledge" be measured by science? No instrument observes that norm as a physical datum. No experiment proves that only experimental results count as knowledge, because interpreting experiments already depends on logic, mathematics, trust in memory, background metaphysics, and the reliability of reason.

That does not humiliate science; it protects science from being misused. The self-grounding test invalidates the totalizing standard, not empirical inquiry. Once the rule fails on its own terms, the question changes: not "must Islam abandon reason?" but "which sources of knowledge are rationally admissible, and how are they ordered?" Islam answers that revelation is not an enemy of reason; sound reason (ʿaql ṣarīḥ) recognizes its own limits, receives true testimony, and does not demand that all reality become a lab object before it may be known.

Land(¹B): the science-only criterion is bounded and self-authorizing exclusion is invalidated; science remains honored as a method, but not enthroned as the only source of knowledge.
