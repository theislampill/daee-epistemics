### ¹B₁[source-status-repair] — source-order repair
Target: direct-observation-only-knowledge-source rule that treats observation as the only admissible source of knowledge.

Operation: source-status-repair.source-order sorts which source can carry which kind of claim: direct observation carries what is immediately perceived; testimony carries knowledge of what another competent witness observed, reported, transmitted, taught, or preserved.

Result/state-change: source-order-repaired; testimony is no longer excluded by being different from sight, because the source function is different rather than defective.

Contribution-to-Land(¹B): This blocks the false source-order collapse because it prevents "not directly observed by me" from becoming "not knowable."

TTP Operation Body:
The direct-observation-only-knowledge-source rule confuses a valid source with the whole source-order. Observation is a powerful source for what is present to the senses, but human knowledge also includes memory, reasoning, inference, and testimony. Testimony is not pretending to be eyesight; it is the ordinary warrant by which one person's knowledge can be responsibly transmitted to another.

If testimony were removed as a knowledge source, ordinary life would collapse into selective skepticism: names, dates, places, language learning, medical reports, court evidence, maps, news, family history, and most specialized knowledge would no longer count unless personally observed. The state change is therefore local and precise: testimony is restored as a real source whose strength depends on witness competence, honesty, number, independence, preservation, and fit with other knowledge, not on being identical to direct observation.

### ¹B₂[M1] — self-grounding test
Target: testimony-exclusion-standard that says "only what I directly observe counts as knowledge."

Operation: M1.self-grounding-test tests whether that standard can authorize its own verdict without borrowing what it denies.

Result/state-change: self-authorizing-standard-invalidated; the exclusion rule cannot be known by direct observation alone and therefore cannot govern all knowledge.

Contribution-to-Land(¹B): This lands the burden because the rule excluding testimony fails by its own criterion while testimony remains bounded by reliability conditions rather than accepted blindly.

TTP Operation Body:
The testimony-exclusion-standard is not itself something directly observed. You do not see with your eyes the universal proposition "only direct observation produces knowledge." That proposition is a philosophical rule about sources of knowledge. So if the rule is applied strictly, it cannot certify itself as knowledge.

The result is not "believe every report." The result is that testimony counts as a genuine knowledge-source when its conditions are met: the witness is positioned to know, the report is intelligible, there is no defeating counterevidence, and the transmission is reliable enough for the claim being carried. Sound reason / ʿaql ṣarīḥ does not flatten testimony into gullibility; it places it in the right order.

Land(¹B): testimony is restored as a bounded knowledge-source, and the direct-observation-only exclusion standard is invalidated as self-authorizing.
