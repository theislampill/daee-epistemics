# Hard-Compound MRP Behavior Smokes

These prompts are the inter-burden MRP proof gate. They are intentionally larger than the
original five small smokes and must not be treated as ordinary format checks.

Passing patched v0.4.3.0 output must show MRP as an interstitial route governor:

Input -> burden nodes -> submove nodes -> Land(Bn) or partial Land(Bn) -> R(H,Delta) ->
MRP pressure slots -> MRP resultant -> graph/field_witness delta -> route ->
next burden, HOLD, LoopBreak, or closure/restoration.

Fail the smoke when MRP appears only after all burdens are already answered.

Required suite coverage:

- `hard1-secularism-partial-hold.txt`: partial landing with HOLD and held graph-bound burdens.
- `hard2-hiddenness-recurse.txt`: RECURSE into a downstream burden after landing an upstream burden.
- `hard3-doubt-churn-loopbreak.txt`: LoopBreak or STOP after doubt-churn, with no proof-stacking.
- `hard4-preemptive-moral-recoil.txt`: structurally licensed pre-voiced recoil dispatched to existing TTP/operator classes.
- `hard5-naturalism-multi-burden.txt`: multi-burden worldview traversal preserving reconstructible node lineage.

have performed.
