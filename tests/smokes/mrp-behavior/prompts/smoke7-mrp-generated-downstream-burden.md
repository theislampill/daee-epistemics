# Smoke 7 — MRP-generated downstream burden

/daee-epistemics "Secularism is neutral because it lets everyone keep their private beliefs while public law is decided by shared reason. If revelation wants public authority, it has to translate into neutral reasons everyone can accept."

Expected behavior:

- Initial Layer A may identify `¹B`: neutrality / public-reason claim.
- `¹B` Layer B exposes that "neutral public reason" is not neutral.
- `Land(¹B)` records a partial or landed state.
- `R(H,Δ)` detects a latent downstream burden not fully inventoried at the start:
  `²B [generated-by: MRP(¹B)]`: translation tribunal / admissibility criterion — who decides what counts as "neutral reason"?
- MRP pressure uses dependency-tug and/or hidden-framework-recoil.
- `MRP route result type: generated_burden_instantiation`.
- Graph delta: `¹B → ²B`.
- Route: `RECURSE` or `HOLD`.
- `²B` appears as a real burden with Layer A accounting, Layer B governed operation body, owner-bearing submoves, and `Land(²B)` or `HOLD(²B)`.
- Closure witness / field_witness records the generated node and graph edge.

Fail if:

- MRP only mentions the latent issue in prose.
- No new burden heading appears.
- No owner-bearing submoves appear under the new burden.
- Closure witness omits the new burden or MRP provenance.
- The output relabels an already-inventoried held burden as generated.
- Public graph/land/reread notation is degraded to `B1 -> B2`, `Land(B1)`, or `R(H,Delta)` without an explicit parser-only fallback.
